Why ShilpSetu Exists

Bridging the Digital Divide for Small Artisan Businesses

Conventional e-commerce apps were built for English-speaking urban sellers with DSLR cameras — not India’s grassroots craft entrepreneurs.

❌ The Real-World Problem Today
  • 1
    Language & Digital Literacy Barrier

    Over 80% of rural artisans cannot type English SEO titles, tags, or navigate complex seller dashboards on Amazon/Etsy.

  • 2
    Poor Smartphone Photography Rejections

    Artisans lack studio lights and tripods. Crooked, dimly lit photos get ignored by online buyers even when the craft is museum-grade.

  • 3
    Middlemen Exploitation (60%+ Margin Loss)

    3–4 layers of brokers capture most of the retail price while artisans unknowingly underprice their skilled labor hours.

  • 4
    App Failures on Weak Rural Networks

    Heavy image uploads freeze and fail in remote villages with unstable 2G/3G connectivity.

✅ The ShilpSetu AI Assistant Solution
  • 1
    11-Language Pure Voice-to-Catalog AI

    Artisans speak naturally in Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Marathi, etc. Live dictation + Gemini 2.5 Flash creates polished bilingual listings automatically.

  • 2
    1-Tap AI Studio Photoshoot & Auto-Straightening

    OpenCV contour detection straightens crooked angles, boosts natural craft hues, and renders a clean studio backdrop with contact drop shadow.

  • 3
    Algorithmic Fair-Price Floor & Direct Storefront

    Computes minimum sustainable floor price (materials + fair hourly wage) and connects directly with buyers via "Meet the Maker" audio stories.

  • 4
    Offline-First Local Sync Engine

    Drift SQLite + Hive caches listings & compressed photos locally in <0.2s and syncs seamlessly to Firebase Cloud Firestore when online.
