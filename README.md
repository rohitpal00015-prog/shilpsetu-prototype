## 🌟 What is ShilpSetu?
**ShilpSetu (कलासेतु / शिल्पसेतु)** is an AI-driven, voice-first e-commerce assistant and dual-sided marketplace designed for India's **7 million+ traditional artisans and rural micro-enterprises**.

Artisans simply **snap a smartphone photo** and **speak in their mother tongue** (11 Indian languages supported):
- **📸 AI Studio Photoshoot & Auto-Straightener:** Fixes crooked/tilted camera shots, removes background clutter, and adds warm studio lighting + soft ground shadows.
- **🎙️ 11-Language Pure Live Voice Typing:** Zero-typing voice cataloging in Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi, Odia, and English.
- **🤖 Gemini 2.5 Flash Bilingual SEO Engine:** Automatically writes English & Hindi titles, craft stories, categories, and tags, with audio read-aloud accessibility.
- **⚖️ Fair-Price Cost Floor Calculator:** Protects raw material costs + skilled artisan labor hours so makers earn **92% direct payout** with zero middleman exploitation.
- **🛍️ Two-Sided Marketplace + "Meet the Maker" Audio Stories:** Seamless 1-tap switching between Seller Portal & Buyer Storefront with UPI/COD checkout and 4-stage live order tracking.
- **✈️ Offline-First 2G/3G Architecture:** Local Drift SQLite + Hive queue syncs automatically to Firebase Cloud Firestore when connectivity returns.
